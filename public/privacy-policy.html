Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Tamson Healthcare Consulting & Staffing ("Tamson Healthcare", "we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form and messages. When you use the contact form or email or call us, we collect your name, email address, organization, and the message you send.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard server information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Please do not send patient information

Our contact form and email are not a secure channel for health information. Please do not include patient names, medical record numbers, or other protected health information (PHI) in a message. If work with you ever involves PHI, it would be handled under a separate written agreement between us and your organization.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss how we can help your organization.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept. Your browser and our hosting provider may store or send small pieces of technical data that are needed to deliver the pages you request. If we ever add analytics or other cookies, we will update this section and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, and only as needed for that purpose. These include the form service that delivers contact form submissions to us (Formspree) and our website hosting provider. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Links to other sites, such as our Facebook page, lead to services with their own privacy practices. This policy does not cover them.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you, manage any resulting work together, and meet legal or record-keeping duties.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for business visitors and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
